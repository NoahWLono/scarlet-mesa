# Touhou: Scarlet Mesa

*Unaligned in San Francisco*

A free, unofficial Touhou Project bullet-hell fan game for Linux and macOS. Play as Reimu across three stages, with nine boss spell patterns, three difficulties, stage practice, and original music. The AI safety dialogue is fictional comedy.

Download the archive for your system and its `.sha256` checksum:

- **Linux x86-64:** extract the `linux-x86_64.tar.gz` archive and run `./scarlet-mesa` from the extracted folder. Keep the `assets` folder beside the executable. SDL2 and the C++ runtime are bundled; desktop and audio libraries come from the system.
- **macOS Apple Silicon and Intel:** extract the `macos-universal.tar.gz` archive and open `Scarlet Mesa.app`. The universal build targets macOS 11 or later. It is ad-hoc signed, not notarized. Right-click **Open** on first launch; if blocked, follow [Apple's Open Anyway instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445) in Privacy & Security.

Move with **Arrow keys / WASD**, shoot with **Z / Space**, hold **Shift** to focus and show the hitbox, and press **X** to bomb. **C** toggles autofire, **Esc** pauses, **M** toggles sound, and **F11** toggles fullscreen. The title menu includes a field manual.
