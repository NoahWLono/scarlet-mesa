Title illustration: title.png and title.bmp
Created for Touhou: Scarlet Mesa - Unaligned in San Francisco, 2026-09-06.
Generated with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool from the original
composition prompt in title-prompt.txt. No official game art was extracted
or used as an input. title.bmp is an RGB format conversion of title.png.

Artwork depicts Reimu Hakurei and Yukari Yakumo from Touhou Project.
Touhou Project and its characters belong to ZUN / Team Shanghai Alice.
This is an unofficial fan work and has no affiliation with its creators.

Native image dimensions: 1672 x 941 pixels.
Composition: quiet dark left area, Golden Gate Bridge and characters at right.

Font atlases: font.bmp, font-bold.bmp, and font-display.bmp

bold builds. Only printable ASCII glyphs are included. JetBrains Mono was

SIL Open Font License 1.1, copied in FONT-LICENSE.txt.
Source font hashes and exact atlas metrics are in font-metadata.json.
font-display.bmp renders the bold face at four times the standard resolution.
